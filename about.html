ABOUT

Built for everyday Kenyan business.

M-Pesa Business Tools is a practical collection of calculators and simple record-keeping tools for entrepreneurs and small businesses in Kenya.

What you can do

Track daily sales and expenses, estimate profit, set sales targets, manage pricing, create customer receipts and understand basic loan repayments.

Simple by design

The app is designed to help business owners make quick decisions without complicated accounting software. Your local records are stored in your browser for the current version of the app.
